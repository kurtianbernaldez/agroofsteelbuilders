About A & G Roof Steel Builders

With over 15 years in steel construction, we deliver roofs and steel structures that are built to last, not just look good on day one. From commercial sheds to detailed residential work, every project is planned, installed, and checked with care.

We coordinate the full package — materials, crews, and timelines — so you have a single team responsible for getting your steel and roofing work done safely, neatly, and on schedule.

Founded in 2009, A & G Roof Steel Builders has grown from a small roofing crew into a full-service steel construction company trusted by businesses and homeowners across the Philippines.
